## Accessing GitHub Copilot (Student Developer Pack)

To get free access to GitHub Copilot for your module work, you need to verify your student status with GitHub. Follow these steps carefully, as the automated verification system is very strict.

**Step 1: Link Your University Email**

* Go to your GitHub account **Settings** > **Emails**.
* Add and verify your Leeds Beckett University email address (ending in `@student.leedsbeckett.ac.uk`).

**Step 2: Apply for the Student Pack**

* Visit [education.github.com/students](https://education.github.com/students?utm_source=gemini).
* Click **Sign up for Student Developer Pack**.
* Select your verified student email address and enter "Leeds Beckett University" as your institution.

**Step 3: Submit Proof of Enrollment**

* You will be prompted to take a photo of your student ID or upload official university documentation.
* **Crucial:** The image must clearly show your name, the university name, and a valid date for the current academic year.
* *Tip:* Ensure the display name on your GitHub profile matches the name on your university ID before applying, or the automated system may reject it.

**Step 4: Enable Copilot**

* Approval usually takes between a few hours and a few days. You will receive a confirmation email once your status is verified.
* Once approved, go to your GitHub account **Settings** > **Copilot**.
* Follow the prompts to enable your free student access. You will then be able to log into GitHub through Visual Studio or CLion to use Copilot directly in your editor.
