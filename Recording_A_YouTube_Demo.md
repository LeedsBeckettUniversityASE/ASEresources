Create a Blank Project.  
Select Record Software Simulation.  
Select full screen or move the bounding box to the part of the screen you want to record.

**How To Make Chapters/Timestamps for your Demo Videos**

Just add timestamps to the YouTube **video description** in this format:

“\<time\> \<chapter name\>” and they’ll automatically become chapters on the video

For example:

1:02 question 1

4:58 question 2

8:23 question 3

For the chapters in the video, the creator has to edit the description of the video to contain timestamps in this format: m:ss \- \[Title\] For example: 0:00 \- Introduction 1:52 \- Chapter 1 4:25 \- Chapter 2 There are some conditions, like the first timecode starting at 0:00, or there being a minimum of three timestamps. These are stated in this link: [https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en-GB](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en-GB)   
But it is a manual process.  
[Video chapters \- YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en-GB)  
Video chapters break up a video into sections, each with an individual preview. Video chapters add info and context to each portion of the video and let you easily rewatch different parts of the video
